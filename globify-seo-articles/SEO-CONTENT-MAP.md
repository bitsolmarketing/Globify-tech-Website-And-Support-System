## 5. Keyword Cannibalization Risk Assessment

| Risk pair | Assessment | Mitigation applied |
|---|---|---|
| Article 1 vs. Article 2 ("AI institute" queries) | **Low risk.** Article 1 targets national intent ("best AI institute in Pakistan"), Article 2 targets city-qualified intent ("...in Faisalabad"). Google treats geo-qualified queries as distinct intent clusters. | Article 2's H1/title always carries "Faisalabad"; Article 1 never optimises for the bare city name. |
| Article 2 vs. Article 4 ("AI course Faisalabad" / "AI institute Faisalabad") | **Moderate risk — same city, adjacent phrasing.** Article 2 is institute-comparison intent (which provider to choose); Article 4 is course-content intent (what will I actually learn). | Kept as secondary keywords in both (marked `*` in Section 2) rather than primary in both. Article 2's H2s frame around "evaluating an institute"; Article 4's H2s frame around "what you'll learn/build." Monitor Search Console query overlap after 90 days; if both rank for the same query with neither in top 10, consider merging or re-scoping. |
| Article 5 vs. Article 2 ("AI education Faisalabad" / "AI institute Faisalabad") | **Low risk.** Article 5 is awareness-stage ("why does this matter locally"), explicitly non-transactional, with no course comparison content. Article 2 is comparison-stage. | Article 5 deliberately excludes fee tables, curriculum breakdowns, or "how to evaluate" checklists — those live only in 2 and 4. |
| Article 3 vs. Article 7 ("AI skills Pakistan" secondary term) | **Low risk.** Article 3 answers "which course/path to learn," Article 7 answers "how do I build a career" (a broader, sequential roadmap). Overlap is in shared secondary vocabulary, not competing H1/primary intent. | Article 3 stays course/path-comparison focused; Article 7 stays roadmap/sequencing focused with a timeline table not present in 3. |
| Article 6 vs. Article 8 (automation + freelancing overlap) | **Low risk.** Article 6 is curriculum-focused (what automation training teaches); Article 8 is monetisation-focused (how to sell any AI skill, automation included, as freelance work). | Article 8 treats automation as one of six services, not its main subject; Article 6 does not cover platform/client-acquisition strategy. |
| Article 9 vs. Article 3/7 (marketing as one "path" vs. dedicated article) | **Low risk.** Article 3 and 7 mention AI marketing briefly as one path among several with a link out; Article 9 is the only article with full marketing-specific depth (SEO/ads/analytics breakdown). | No duplication of the 9-topic marketing breakdown outside Article 9. |

**Overall assessment: no critical cannibalization risk.** The one pairing worth monitoring post-publish is Article 2 vs. Article 4 — recommend checking Google Search Console query-level performance at the 90-day mark.

## 6. E-E-A-T Compliance Check

- ✅ No article claims Globify Tech is "officially #1" or independently ranked — every ranking-adjacent claim is explicitly framed as unverified market absence of a ranking body.
- ✅ All Globify Tech statistics (8,500+ students, 92% completion, 76% earning within 6 months, 45+ hiring partners, 18-student batch cap, 80% hands-on) are labeled "self-reported" or "Globify Tech, self-reported" at first use in every article that cites them (Articles 1, 2, 10).
- ✅ All national/market statistics are sourced to named, dated, linkable third parties (HEC, Dawn, Arab News, PIDE, NAVTTC, SBP/Daily Times, The Nation, WEF, Upwork, Fiverr/Accio, DHL Discover) — no invented statistics anywhere in the campaign.
- ✅ No guaranteed income, job placement percentage-as-guarantee, salary guarantee, HEC recognition claim, or university affiliation is asserted for Globify Tech anywhere.
- ✅ No fabricated testimonials were added — the existing testimonial dataset in the codebase (`src/lib/content.ts`) was treated as pre-approved real content and was not drawn into these SEO articles at all, to keep every claim in this campaign independently sourced or clearly self-reported.
- ✅ Salary/income ranges cited (e.g., Article 4's career table) are explicitly labeled as "indicative market ranges, not guarantees."
- ✅ Every article distinguishes "Globify Tech reports/states" language from independently verified fact.

- Revisit Globify Tech's self-reported stats (8,500+/92%/76%/45+) if the institute updates `src/lib/content.ts` — these articles will need a factual refresh in sync with the site's own published numbers.
