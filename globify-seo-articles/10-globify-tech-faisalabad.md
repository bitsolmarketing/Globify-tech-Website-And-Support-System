## Article

Globify Tech Institute has operated from Faisalabad since 2019, built specifically around a gap its founder kept seeing in Pakistan's technical education market: graduates who could pass an exam but had never shipped anything real. This article lays out, plainly and without inflated claims, who Globify Tech is, what it actually teaches, and what a student's experience looks like — with Globify Tech's own reported figures clearly marked as such throughout.

### Who Globify Tech Is

Globify Tech Institute (legally Globify Tech Institute (Pvt) Ltd) is a practical, career-focused technology institute based in Faisalabad, Punjab. It was founded by **Usman Rafiq**, an AI automation consultant with a background as a product engineer, who started the institute after eight years of building software and marketing systems for clients in Pakistan, the UAE and the UK. Today he leads the institute's AI & Automation track and personally reviews the capstone project of every AI batch.

The institute's campus is located at **2nd Floor, Kohinoor Plaza, Jaranwala Road, Faisalabad, Punjab 38000**, open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. It began, by its own account, with a single classroom, two trainers and eleven students in its first web development batch in 2019, and has since expanded its facilities to include dedicated design and video editing labs.

### What Students Can Learn

Globify Tech's course catalogue spans three categories, each led by a named instructor with current, verifiable professional experience:

**AI & Development**
[Full Stack Development with AI](/courses/full-stack-development-with-ai) — a 3-month, 12-week programme teaching HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Next.js, Node.js and databases, with AI coding assistants (GitHub Copilot, Claude) introduced as a professional tool once students can already read and judge code. Taught by **Hassan Mehmood**, a full-stack engineer with production experience across fintech, logistics and e-commerce companies.

**Marketing & Business**
- [Digital Media Marketing with AI](/courses/digital-media-marketing-with-ai) — SEO, Meta Ads, Google Ads, funnels and AI content workflows, built around live campaigns with institute-funded ad spend
- [Social Media Marketing with AI](/courses/social-media-marketing-with-ai) — organic growth, paid social, short-form video and AI-assisted content creation
- [TikTok Shop](/courses/tiktok-shop) — seller and affiliate operations, product research, live selling and creator partnerships
- [Facebook Automation & Monetization](/courses/facebook-automation-and-monetization) — page growth, Messenger and workflow automation, and monetisation strategy

These four are led by **Ayesha Siddiqui** (Head of Digital Marketing, Google Ads and Meta Blueprint certified) and **Bilal Ahmed** (E-Commerce & Career Services Lead), depending on the specific programme.

**Design & Media**
- [Graphic Designing](/courses/graphic-designing) — Photoshop, Illustrator, typography and brand identity, taught through weekly critique
- [Video Editing: Beginner to Pro](/courses/video-editing) — Premiere Pro, After Effects, colour, sound and short-form editing

Both led by **Zainab Khan**, Head of Design, Adobe Certified with a decade of brand and product design experience for regional and Faisalabad-based textile and FMCG clients.

### AI Education at Globify Tech

AI is not confined to a single course at Globify Tech — it runs through the AI & Development track directly, and appears as an accelerant inside the marketing and automation programmes (AI-assisted ad copy, content drafting, and campaign reporting). The institute's approach, consistently across programmes, is to teach the underlying skill first and introduce AI tooling once a student has the judgement to evaluate what the AI produces — a sequencing choice the institute states is deliberate rather than a limitation of the curriculum.

### What the Institute Says Differentiates Its Approach

Beyond the specific courses, Globify Tech describes six things it treats as core to how it operates, again presented here as the institute's own stated positioning rather than independently verified claims:

- **Practical over theoretical** — the institute states roughly 80% of contact hours across programmes are hands-on rather than lecture-based.
- **Live budgets and real accounts** — marketing students use institute-funded ad spend rather than hypothetical case studies; development students deploy to production rather than submitting files.
- **Weekly accountability** — structured weekly submissions and critique, which the institute connects to its reported 92% completion rate.
- **Career support past the certificate** — CV clinics, mock interviews, portfolio reviews and employer introductions continuing after the course ends.
- **Published, not promised, outcomes** — the institute states it publishes its real figures rather than making guaranteed-income or guaranteed-placement claims.
- **Built around Faisalabad specifically** — fee structures, batch timings and course selection designed around the city's students and its textile and export-driven business base, rather than a generic national curriculum applied without local adjustment.

### Practical Projects, Not Just Lectures

Every Globify Tech programme is built around a capstone project rather than a written exam:

- Development students deploy a full-stack application with a public URL and a GitHub repository
- Marketing students run a live campaign with institute-funded ad spend and produce a client-ready performance report
- Design students complete a full brand identity with a guidelines document
- Automation students build a working, documented automation solving a real business use case

The institute reports that roughly 80% of contact hours across its programmes are hands-on rather than lecture-based ([Globify Tech, self-reported](/why-choose-us)).

### Career-Oriented Learning and Career Support

Beyond the certificate, Globify Tech's stated career support includes CV clinics, mock interviews, portfolio reviews, and introductions to what the institute describes as 45+ hiring partner agencies and companies, plus freelance profile setup on platforms like Fiverr and Upwork ([Globify Tech, self-reported](/why-choose-us)). Students with strong project work may also be eligible for a supervised internship on live client projects, typically six to eight weeks, which the institute states has led to some full-time offers and ongoing freelance retainers in past batches.

It's worth stating this plainly: Globify Tech does not guarantee job placement, and is explicit that no institute honestly can. What it commits to is the support infrastructure around a student's own job or client search.

### The Faisalabad Campus and Student Experience

Batches are capped at 18 students, a deliberate limit the institute states exists specifically so an instructor can review every student's work weekly rather than a subset of a larger class. Classes run in morning, evening and weekend timings across On-Campus, Live Online and Hybrid modes, designed around Faisalabad students who are frequently balancing a job, university, or a family business alongside their course. The on-campus lab, including licensed design software and development machines, is available free to enrolled students throughout their course, including outside class hours — specifically to remove hardware as a barrier for students who don't have a suitable personal laptop.

### A Typical Student's Journey Through a Programme

To make the experience concrete: a new student typically begins with an admissions conversation confirming the right course and batch timing for their goals and schedule, followed by an orientation session covering how the lab, portal and weekly review process work. The first few weeks of any programme focus on fundamentals — deliberately slower-paced than later modules, so students without prior experience aren't left behind early. From roughly the midpoint of the course, sessions shift toward more independent, brief-driven project work with weekly instructor critique, building toward the final capstone project in the last two to three weeks. Throughout, students retain access to recorded sessions for the duration of the course plus three months, specifically so a missed class or a difficult module doesn't permanently set someone back.

### Verified Certificate and Alumni Access

Globify Tech states that course completion certificates carry a unique verification code employers can check, along with a LinkedIn-ready credential link. Graduates also retain access to the institute's alumni community after finishing, which the institute describes as a channel where job openings, client leads and technical questions circulate among former students — intended to extend the value of the course past the final day of class rather than ending support at graduation.

### Industry-Focused Curriculum

The institute states its curriculum is revised on an ongoing basis rather than fixed once and left unchanged — a claim borne out by the relatively recent addition of its AI & Automation-focused content, which the institute describes as one of the earlier moves among Faisalabad institutes toward teaching production AI-assisted workflows rather than AI theory alone. Instructors are described as maintaining current commercial work alongside teaching specifically so the syllabus reflects what the industry is doing now, not what it was doing when the instructor was trained.

### Who Globify Tech Is a Good Fit For

Based on everything above, Globify Tech is worth shortlisting specifically if you want a practical, project-based programme over a purely theoretical one, value having a named instructor with current commercial experience rather than an anonymous trainer, are based in or able to attend Faisalabad (on-campus, live online, or hybrid), and are looking for a capstone-driven course that ends in something you can show a client or employer rather than a written exam. It is less likely to be the right fit if you specifically need a formal, HEC-recognised degree credential, or if you're seeking pure machine learning research training rather than applied, industry-facing skills — for those goals, a university BS Computer Science or BS Artificial Intelligence programme is the more appropriate route.
