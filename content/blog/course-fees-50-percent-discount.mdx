## What happens after you enrol

Worth knowing before you commit, because the week after payment is where most institutes go quiet.

**Within one working day**, admissions calls to confirm your batch, timing, delivery mode and start date, and answers anything outstanding. If you enrolled online, this is also when we verify your details.

**Before your first session**, you receive your student portal login, the joining details for your batch, and a short preparation note — usually a software installation list and one or two things to read. Nothing that takes more than an hour.

**In week one**, your trainer sets expectations directly: how submissions work, when they are due, what the critique format is, and what happens if you fall behind. We would rather have that conversation on day one than in week six.

**From week two onward**, you submit work weekly and it gets reviewed by name. This is the mechanism the whole programme rests on. If you miss two submissions in a row, your trainer will contact you — not as a formality, but because that is the point at which students quietly disappear, and catching it early is why our completion rate is what it is.

**In the final module**, career services takes over: CV clinic, portfolio review, mock interviews and freelance profile setup, plus introductions into our hiring-partner network where there is a fit.

## Choosing if you are still unsure

Two quick filters that resolve most indecision.

**Filter one: your timeline.** If you need income within three to four months, choose from the shorter, faster-return programmes — Canva Mastery, Office Automation, Video Editing, Graphic Designing or Amazon VA. If you can invest six to ten months before earning properly, Web Development and AI have far higher ceilings. This is a sequencing decision, not a compromise; plenty of our students do the fast one first and the ambitious one a year later.

**Filter two: your weekly hours.** Look at your actual week and find the hours the course requires — five to eight, depending on the programme. Write down which hours on which days. If you cannot find them, take a shorter course rather than an ambitious one you will abandon in week five. That single act of honesty predicts outcomes better than anything else we could assess.

If both filters still leave you with two or three options, that is what the free counselling call is for.
